## Integrity decisions

1. No marketplace crawler, OCR, URL fetcher or reverse image search is part of the product. The extension reads one listing only when the buyer clicks.
2. No research listing, label, adjudication, participant, consent or trial row is generated by the system.
3. Scores are annotated-risk estimates, never proof of fraud, and the low band is never called safe.
4. Development scores, when stubs are used, carry the label `dev-stub-unvalidated` and a permanent warning.

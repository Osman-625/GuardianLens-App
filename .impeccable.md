## Design Context

Source of truth for binding rules and locked copy: `docs/spec/04_Design_Brief_UI_UX.md` (sections 1, 2, 3.4). This file records the polish direction chosen on 2026-10-15. If the two conflict on a binding rule, the brief wins.

### Users
Non-expert buyers in Malaysia checking a Mudah.my or Carousell listing **before paying**. They are mid-decision, often on a phone or in a narrow browser side panel, slightly anxious, and not technical. Job to be done: "Should I be careful with this seller, and what exactly should I verify myself?" The site's `/assess` page is the manual fallback; the extension side panel is the main surface. A second audience is the FYP supervisor and examiners, who judge craft, accessibility and honesty of the interface.

### Brand Personality
Plain-spoken, measured, accountable. Register is "a lab report written in plain words": calm, factual, slightly institutional. Nothing alarmist (a High band is serious information, not a siren). Nothing celebratory (a Low band is "no strong warning signs found", never "safe to buy"). No mascots, no shield-and-padlock, no money or handcuff imagery.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Refine within the brief.** Keep the navy and blue on cool grey palette, white bordered cards, ring dial for the score, borders before shadows. Raise craft; do not re-theme.
- **Theme: light only.** Daytime browsing on marketplaces; the brief defines no dark theme. Do not add one.
- **Typeface: one distinctive, locally bundled face** replacing the never-loaded Inter. Must have Latin glyphs for Malay text, a variable weight axis, and tabular figures for the score. Bundled (not remote): Chrome MV3 blocks remote fonts in the extension.
  - **Chosen (2026-10-15): Public Sans** (`@fontsource-variable/public-sans`, Latin subset only, about 27 KB, wired once via `@font-face` in `shared/src/styles.css`). Libre Franklin was tried first and rejected: its `tabular-nums` changed no digit widths in Chromium.
- **Anti-references:** consumer security products (shields, glow, red sirens), generic SaaS dashboards (hero-metric grids, identical icon cards), gradient text, side-stripe borders, glassmorphism.
- Colour is information, never decoration: risk colours always travel with text and an icon (binding rule 4).

### Design Principles
1. **Calm is the feature.** Every high-impact moment (score reveal, stage progress) is quiet and exact. No looping attention-seeking motion near the score.
2. **Honest uncertainty beats polish.** Unavailable signals stay named and framed as unknown. No false precision: integer scores only.
3. **One system, two surfaces.** Change tokens and shared components in `shared/src` first so the site and the side panel move together. Page-specific CSS only for layout.
4. **Accessible by construction.** WCAG 2.1 AA, visible focus, 44px targets on touch, labelled controls, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, text/number/icon on every risk state.
5. **Smallest diff that lifts quality.** Prefer fixing a token or a shared class over per-page overrides. Delete dead CSS rather than layering on top.

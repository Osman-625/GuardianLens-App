## Design Context

Source of truth for binding rules: `docs/spec/04_Design_Brief_UI_UX.md` (section 1). This file records the visual direction chosen on 2026-10-15 and revised on 2026-11-02. If the two conflict on a binding rule, the brief wins. Where the brief and the build differ on purpose, `docs/CHANGES_FROM_SPEC.md` says so.

### Users
Non-expert buyers in Malaysia checking a Mudah.my or Carousell listing **before paying**. They are mid-decision, often on a phone or in a narrow browser side panel, slightly anxious, and not technical. Job to be done: "Should I be careful with this seller, and what exactly should I verify myself?" The extension side panel is the main surface; the website's `/assess` form is the manual fallback and the home of the full explanation. A second audience is the FYP supervisor and examiners, who judge craft, accessibility and honesty of the interface.

### Brand Personality
Plain-spoken, measured, accountable. Register is "a report written in plain words": calm, factual, slightly institutional. Nothing alarmist (a High band is serious information, not a siren). Nothing celebratory (a Low band is "no strong warning signs found", never "safe to buy"). No mascots, no shield-and-padlock, no money or handcuff imagery.

### Aesthetic Direction (revised 2026-11-02)
- **One idea: the ring.** The logo is a lens ring with one arc lit, and the score dial is the same shape. The mark and the verdict share one form. The ring is the only motif; nothing else decorates.
- **Ground and ink.** Ink navy (`#0F1B2D`) on warm paper (`#F5F4F0`), white bordered cards, one blue (`#1D4ED8`) for links, focus and the lit arc. The main buttons are navy, not blue. Risk colours (green, amber, red) appear only on the score, the band chip and history scores, always with an icon and words.
- **Theme: light only.** Daytime browsing on marketplaces; the brief defines no dark theme.
- **Type: two bundled faces.** Newsreader (variable, Latin) for titles only; Public Sans (variable, Latin, tabular figures for the score) for everything people read or fill in. Both are bundled because Chrome MV3 blocks remote fonts. Wired once in `shared/src/styles.css`.
- **Surfaces.** Borders, not shadows: nothing at rest has a shadow, and the sticky submit bar is the one floating surface. Radius 12 on cards, 10 on buttons, 8 on inputs. The three checks on a result sit in one bordered group, not three separate cards.
- **No eyebrows.** No small uppercase label above a heading; the heading carries itself.
- **Anti-references:** consumer security products (shields, glow, red sirens), generic SaaS dashboards (hero-metric grids, identical icon cards), gradient text, side-stripe borders, glassmorphism.

### Words
- **One vocabulary:** check (the act), result (the output), listing, seller details (what the buyer types). The three checks are named Photos, Wording and Seller and price. The report and the code call them visual, textual and behavioural; the names are set in one place, `shared/src/copy.ts`.
- **Method names stay hidden.** Buyer-facing text never names a model, a model bundle, a training set or a timing. The API still sends them. The cards and the explanation page hide them behind one switch, `SHOW_MODEL_DETAILS` in `shared/src/copy.ts` (off); the elements stay in the page, hidden.
- **The test-mode warning is the exception.** While the API serves placeholder scores it says so, in words, on every result. Hiding that would be false.
- Short sentences; one fact per sentence; a control names its action ("Check listing", "Capture the seller's page").

### Design Principles
1. **Calm is the feature.** Every high-impact moment (score reveal, stage progress) is quiet and exact. One short entrance for the result cards, no looping motion near the score.
2. **Honest uncertainty beats polish.** Unavailable signals stay named and framed as unknown. No false precision: integer scores only.
3. **One system, two surfaces.** Change tokens and shared components in `shared/src` first so the site and the side panel move together. Page-specific CSS only for layout.
4. **Accessible by construction.** WCAG 2.1 AA, visible focus, 44px targets on touch, labelled controls, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, text/number/icon on every risk state.
5. **Smallest diff that lifts quality.** Prefer fixing a token or a shared class over per-page overrides. Delete dead CSS rather than layering on top.
6. **Say it once.** A status that the page already states (the banner says the page was read) is not repeated beside every field.

# GuardianLens User Study Consent Form, Draft

**Status:** Draft only. Do not recruit or run sessions until open item O-3 confirms the INTI ethics or approval process and the supervisor approves this form.

## Study purpose

This study evaluates whether an explained fraud-risk assessment changes how accurately buyers judge Malaysian consumer-to-consumer marketplace listings.

## What participation involves

Participants complete a short set of listing-judgement tasks under unaided and assisted conditions, record confidence after each decision, and answer a short questionnaire. No payment, marketplace login, transaction, or contact with a seller is required.

## Data collected

The study records a pseudonymous participant code, assigned counterbalancing group, judgement, correctness, decision time, confidence, and questionnaire responses. Names, phone numbers, email addresses, marketplace usernames, account numbers, and precise locations must not be entered into the research database.

## Risks and limits

The prototype provides decision support and may be wrong. Study listings are controlled research examples and must not be used as advice for a real purchase.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. A participant may stop before completing the session without giving a reason. The approved withdrawal and retention wording must be inserted after O-3 and O-4 are resolved.
